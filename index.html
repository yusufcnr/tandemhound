Who we serve

Owners of large and giant-breed dogs, mostly in the United States, whose dogs struggle with mobility because of age, hip or joint problems, or recovery after surgery.

Senior dogs

Labradors, Goldens, Shepherds and other big breeds slowing down with age.

Hip & joint issues

Dogs with arthritis or hip dysplasia that need help on stairs and getting up.

Post-surgery recovery

Dogs that need a steady, controlled lift while they heal.

What we make

Stairs, the car, getting up from the floor. When a dog's back legs weaken, everyday moments turn into a lift. A towel under the belly is hard on the dog and hard on your back.

Support at both ends

A front piece for the chest and a rear piece for the hips.

Handles on the back

A dedicated grip for both hands instead of lifting under the belly.

Sizes M, L, XL

For 45–100 lb dogs. Choose by weight, then confirm with a chest measurement.
